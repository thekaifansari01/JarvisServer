About the Builder

The Builder Behind JARVIS

Built by passion, fueled by late nights, and engineered to redefine personal computing — on hardware most AI tools assume you don't have.

Hello, I'm Kaif Ansari.

I am an 18-year-old developer from a commerce background, currently in my first year of BCA. I built JARVIS entirely solo — without a team, without funding, and without a formal computer science background in high school.

The idea for JARVIS was born out of frustration. Existing AI tools were either basic chatbots that forgot context within minutes, or complex enterprise tools that couldn't interact safely with a personal OS. I wanted something that felt real — a system that could autonomously edit code without line-drift, read my emails, manage my calendar, and control my Android phone via Termux SSH, all while asking for permission before doing anything irreversible.

What makes this build unusual is the hardware it was engineered on: an Intel i5-3210M (2012) with 4 GB RAM. No cloud VMs, no beefy workstations. Every optimization — from using cloud LLM providers by default to picking bge-small embeddings and ONNX-based RapidOCR — was a forced decision, not a luxury. Building a Dual-Brain architecture with lifelong memory on 4 GB RAM took countless sleepless nights and endless cups of coffee. But seeing JARVIS execute its first flawless multi-step engineering task — and adapt mid-run when I changed my mind — made every single error log worth it.
